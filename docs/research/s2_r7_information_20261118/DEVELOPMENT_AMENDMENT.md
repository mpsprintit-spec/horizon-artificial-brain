# Decisions before confirmatory training

All development data and failed learning curves are retained. Seeds 37 and 211, 128/256/512/1024/2048 serial prospective exposures, D0-D4 raw/activation/metric audits, fresh unseen/shifted operations and optimizer controls were completed before this decision. H1 failed development: neither D nor E reaches the frozen improvement/F1 requirement on independent seen data. H2/H3 are closed. Final cannot reopen them.

Final method/budget: all A-G comparisons, six specified seeds, 512 prospective experiences per seed, ordinary 64 Fit +64 Check native lessons. D/E/F also receive 128 distinct contribution and 128 distinct retrospective examples. These auxiliary budgets are reported separately. Source history is rebuilt from R2 via D32 on new data in every seed; independently IID-trained matched history controls are added. Final remains diagnostic validation, not checkpoint selection or a search for PASS. No exposure growth after final.

The relation-preserving codec fixes the 64/64 factual raw mean aliases. On the identical controlled pairs, 63/64 still collide exactly in the existing native activation representation; none collide in metric representation. D2/D3/D4 do not restore history access simply by adding coordinates: the existing basis does not read those relocated channels. This is not a proof that 32 features are mathematically insufficient.

Fresh conditioning probes reproduce the dangerous metric-to-activation coefficient transfer on development seed 211. A Fit/Check-admissible two-stage workspace path can improve Check while unseen RMSE rises to about .07577. Independently fitting the new mode from zero gives about .002223; standardized bounded ridge gives about .002219 on the same new holdout. These are controlled uninstalled optimizer workspaces, not a native utility PASS and not fitting to R6 final seed 211. R6 itself is still PARTIAL.

Native repair V3 independently fits standardized coefficients for each mode with fixed ridge .01 and signed bounds, retaining all original admission/retention rules. Legacy V1/V2 semantics are unchanged for exact historical replay. Its efficacy is tested separately from the codec and the curriculum. Source/code/analysis are frozen before six-seed training.

Development V1 taught retrospective auxiliary knowledge before the main utility lessons; final teaches contribution first and retrospective after signed/context/pair utility lessons. A controlled order study on both development seeds verifies bit-exact prospective forecasts before/after that retrospective lesson and versus the archived V1 512-example forecasts. V1 raw journals/curves remain V1, including retrospective measurements; no historical evidence is rewritten. The development runner retains V1 ordering for reproduction, while confirmatory uses the frozen ordered variant.

Final utility: separate native operations for seen, unseen and shifted conditions, 16 streams x16 operations x2 interventions. All utility forecasts are synced to a durable ledger before factual native Step. All interventions from an operation and all operations in a stream share their Fit/Check assignment. Final seeds have independent acquisition RNG streams; hierarchical bootstrap therefore samples streams independently within each sampled seed. IID controls are trained and evaluated on separate independent-generator streams. Retention RNG families are disjoint from all utility/history holdouts.

Legacy new-distribution R6 absolute limits are loaded at full precision from its authenticated RETENTION_LOCK, not rounded report values. R7 additionally reports a preregistered paired source/candidate RMSE ratio upper CI95 <=1.05 on identical final inputs. This statistical diagnostic does not overturn R6, remove the historical .01766457 cutoff, or waive strict S1 shift 0, A-E1, history, calibration or persistence.

Offline raw/activation/metric bounded, ridge and polynomial probes are diagnostic research objects only. Their Fit-only information access and all failures are disclosed; post-outcome analytical identity is separate. No oracle, polynomial function, per-path product or utility formula is inserted into the native codec. The existing native output range is unchanged, and a near-zero forecast fails F1/gain criteria when it cannot distinguish outcomes.

All intermediate checkpoints are retained losslessly; some development checkpoint JSONs are individually Zstandard-compressed with original-byte SHA256 mapping. Full WAL and source bytes are preserved. Compression is storage encoding, not evidence reduction or a change of numerical precision.
