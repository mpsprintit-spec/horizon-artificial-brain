# Requirements for later native adaptive representation research

S2-R3 implements no adaptive controller. Fixed-budget diagnostic copies and
offline statistics do not become a second Brain or a runtime decision engine.
The following are hypotheses for later native-policy learning, not established
Horizon abilities.

| Action | Required neural evidence | Actual gain measurement | Cost and retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reuse | Existing feature activity separates the residual context; signed causal readout ablation shows contribution | Same-budget independent cases, admitted readout change versus incumbent; uncertainty and per-stream gain | Search/transaction time and RAM; old-head and native-policy preservation |
| Growth | Residual remains after reuse; a candidate carries variation not represented in old outputs; fit provenance independent of Check | Held-out improvement and feature/readout ablation, replicated across schedules | New nodes/paired synapses, bytes, inference and learning p95; S1/A–E1 and restart gates |
| Reorganize synapses | Useful incoming information exists but is saturated, poorly connected or absent from the active readout | Controlled small native update versus same-budget growth/reuse; temporal positive control | Update/search cost; shared-head dependency closure and strict observation-matched retention before admission |
| Consolidate | Stable redundant activity/contribution across contexts and time; repeated ablation equivalence, not correlation alone | Independent equivalence interval within a preset loss budget; joint and individual ablations | Actual RAM/latency/bytes saved; descendant and old-task effects; reversible persisted proposal |
| Prune | Sustained lack of contribution plus joint dependency audit; feature might be silent yet useful elsewhere | Independent held-out equivalence across all retained skills and shifted contexts; no single-test deletion | Saved computation/storage must exceed audit/recovery cost; rollback and WAL lineage remain available |
| Retain current graph | No robust beneficial structural proposal under the current information/experience budget | Compare no-change with update/growth on independent outcomes; uncertainty can justify more observation | Avoid unused state; preserve existing knowledge and measure ongoing maintenance cost |

## Experience needed for native operation policy and causal credit

A later experiment would record pre-action neural state/activity, residual
uncertainty and feature dependency signals; the actually chosen native action;
measured before/after predictions on independent experience; actual allocation,
latency and storage deltas; delayed retention/restart outcomes; and provenance
linking those consequences to the structural operation. Credit must come from
the observed change attributable to that action, with matched alternatives or
randomized contrasts where feasible. Count growth only after an admitted native
transaction. The no-change option is a genuine comparison.

A possible research utility is predictive gain minus measured resource costs
and retention damage. Its coefficients and constraints must be registered and
tested, not disguised as a learned policy. Shared neural representations,
native eligibility and causal credit would learn from those consequences on
the same substrate. No external capacity AI, new cognitive engine, target-fed
feature constructor or hand-coded answer rule is licensed by this document.

Before autonomous action, collect replicated evidence for reuse versus growth
versus small reorganization; establish calibrated benefit uncertainty, resource
instrumentation and a reversible neural transaction; validate every affected
head beyond the current bounded witness bank. Pruning/consolidation especially
need a broader retention battery. One 32-versus-40 comparison cannot determine
an optimal universal capacity or resource-price coefficient.
